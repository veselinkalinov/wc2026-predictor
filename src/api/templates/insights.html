Squad Rotation & Friendlies

Friendlies are included to provide volume, but teams often rotate squads or play at sub-optimal intensity, introducing noise to rolling form features.

info

Cold Start for Rolling Windows

The first few matches of the dataset fall into a cold-start period for form (window=5) and goals (window=10) averages. These are filled with defaults (e.g. 1.2 goals) until histories compile.

info

Static ELO Starting Parameters

Initial ELO rating is set statically to 1500 for all teams at their date of entry, which slightly penalizes historically elite teams in their initial games.

Symmetric Evaluation

For neutral ground tournament matches, the predictor runs two inferences by swapping the Home and Away order, and then averages the inverted probabilities. This ensures ordering bias is mathematically eliminated from group stage and knockout predictions.
